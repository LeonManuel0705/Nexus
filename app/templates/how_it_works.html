How It Works

From speech to text in seconds, powered by state-of-the-art AI running entirely on your device.

1

Capture Your Voice

Press the record button and start speaking. VoiceNotes captures high-quality audio from your microphone with intelligent noise detection and voice activity monitoring.

  • Real-time audio waveform visualization
  • Voice activity detection (VAD)
  • Works with any microphone
2

AI Transcription

Your audio is processed by Whisper, OpenAI's state-of-the-art speech recognition model, running entirely on your device. No cloud processing, no data uploads.

  • Whisper AI for high accuracy
  • Real-time streaming output
  • German and English support
3

Intelligent Enhancement

Your transcription is automatically enhanced using local AI models. Grammar is corrected, punctuation is improved, and context-specific terms are recognized.

  • LanguageTool grammar checking
  • MLX AI on Apple Silicon
  • Context-aware corrections
4

Voice Filtering (Optional)

Need to isolate a specific speaker? Create a voice profile by recording a short sample. VoiceNotes will then filter recordings to include only that voice.

  • Speaker recognition AI
  • Include or exclude mode
  • Adaptive threshold tuning
5

Organize & Export

Your notes are automatically saved and can be organized into folders with tags. Export anytime to Word, Markdown, or plain text for use in other applications.

  • Hierarchical folder structure
  • Full-text search
  • Multiple export formats
