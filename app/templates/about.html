About VoiceNotes

Built for privacy, designed for productivity.

Our Mission

VoiceNotes was created with a simple goal: provide powerful voice transcription that respects your privacy. In a world where most services send your data to the cloud, we believe you should have complete control over your information.

Every word you speak is processed locally on your device. No accounts, no subscriptions, no data collection. Your notes belong to you.

100% Offline

All processing happens on your device. Your voice never leaves your computer. No internet connection required after initial setup.

Technology

VoiceNotes combines state-of-the-art AI technologies to deliver accurate, real-time transcription:

Whisper

OpenAI's advanced speech recognition model, running locally via faster-whisper for optimized performance.

MLX

Apple's machine learning framework for fast inference on Apple Silicon. Powers context-aware grammar correction.

LanguageTool

Open-source grammar and spell checker with support for German and English, running completely offline.

SpeechBrain

Speaker recognition for voice filtering. Identify and isolate specific speakers in recordings.

Why Offline?

We chose an offline-first approach for several important reasons:

  • Privacy: Your voice contains sensitive information. It should never leave your device without your explicit consent.
  • Speed: No network latency means faster transcription and a more responsive experience.
  • Reliability: Works anywhere, anytime. No internet connection required after initial setup.
  • Free Forever: No recurring costs, no usage limits. Once installed, it's yours.
