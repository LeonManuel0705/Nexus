Powerful Features

Everything you need for professional voice transcription, built with privacy in mind.

Real-time Transcription

Watch your words appear on screen as you speak. No waiting for processing - see your transcription build live with minimal latency.

Voice Filter*

Isolate specific speakers from recordings. Perfect for lectures where you only want to capture the teacher's voice.*

Offline-First

All processing happens locally on your device. No internet connection required, no data leaves your computer.

AI-Enhanced Accuracy

Context-aware grammar correction powered by local AI. Automatically fixes common transcription errors and improves readability.

Multi-Language Support

Full support for German and English with automatic language detection. Switch languages on the fly during recording.

Multiple Export Formats

Export your notes as Word documents, Markdown files, or plain text. Perfect for any workflow or documentation system.

Folder Organization

Organize your notes with a hierarchical folder structure. Create nested folders for courses, projects, or any categorization you need.

Tags & Search

Add tags to your notes for quick filtering. Full-text search helps you find any note instantly across your entire collection.

Adaptive Learning

The system learns from your feedback to optimize transcription parameters for each speaker, improving accuracy over time.

AI Summarization

Get instant AI-generated summaries of your notes. Extract key points and main ideas from long transcriptions with a single click.

Topic Detection

Automatic topic detection across 16 subject categories including Math, Physics, Chemistry, Biology, and more. Perfect for organizing study notes.

Auto-Tagging

Let AI automatically tag your notes based on detected topics. Keep your notes organized without manual effort.
